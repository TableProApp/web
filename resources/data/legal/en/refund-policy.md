Every paid plan can be refunded within {refundDays} days: Starter and Team, monthly, yearly and one-time. The {refundDays} days start on the date of purchase, or, for a yearly subscription, on the date of its most recent renewal. The plans themselves are on the [pricing page](/pricing#refunds).

## How to ask for a refund {#request}

Email [{email}](mailto:{email}) from the address you bought with, or include your license key, and tell us which purchase you want refunded. Eligible refunds are processed within 5 business days and go back to the original payment method.

Licenses are sold by {merchant}, our merchant of record, so the refund is issued through {merchant}. {merchant} may also issue refunds under its own buyer terms.

## What happens to the license {#license}

After a refund, the license key is suspended. Each Mac stops using the paid features the next time it checks the license with our server, at most {revalidateDays} days later. The rest of the Mac app keeps working as before, and the data on your Mac is not touched.

## Subscriptions {#subscriptions}

A refund covers the payment you ask about. To stop a monthly or yearly plan renewing, cancel it in [your account](/account?locale=en) or in [{merchant}'s customer portal]({portal}); a cancelled plan is not charged again.

After the {refundDays} days, cancelling stops the next renewal, and the license keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for. Time already paid for is not refunded.

## Chargebacks and payment disputes {#chargebacks}

Please contact us before you open a chargeback or payment dispute. A dispute opened without contacting us first leads to the license key being suspended.

## Your legal rights {#legal-rights}

This policy does not limit any right you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you.
