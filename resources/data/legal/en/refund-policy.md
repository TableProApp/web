Every paid plan can be refunded within {refundDays} days: Starter and Team, monthly, yearly and one-time. The {refundDays} days start on the date of purchase, and each monthly or yearly renewal can be refunded within {refundDays} days of its charge. The plans themselves are on the [pricing page](/pricing#refunds).

## How to ask for a refund {#request}

Refund an order yourself in Billing in [your account]({account}), which lists every order you can still refund. A Starter order is refunded by its buyer, and a Team order by the team's owner. The refund is the full amount of the order, and it goes back to the original payment method.

For an order Billing does not list, such as a change in the number of seats or a purchase whose license is in an offline file, which cannot be taken back, email [{email}](mailto:{email}) from the address you bought with and tell us which purchase you want refunded. Eligible refunds are processed within 5 business days.

Licenses are sold by {merchant}, our merchant of record, so the refund is issued through {merchant}. {merchant} may also issue refunds under its own buyer terms.

## What happens to the license {#license}

After a full refund, the license ends at once. Each Mac stops using the paid features the next time TablePro checks in with our server; one that stays offline stops at most {offlineDays} days later, or when its offline file expires. Versions before TablePro accounts stop at their next license check, at most {revalidateDays} days later. The rest of the Mac app keeps working as before, and the data on your Mac is not touched.

## Subscriptions {#subscriptions}

A refund covers the payment you ask about, and a full refund of a subscription payment also ends the subscription. To stop a monthly or yearly plan renewing without a refund, cancel it in [your account]({account}) or in [{merchant}'s customer portal]({portal}); a cancelled plan is not charged again.

After the {refundDays} days, cancelling stops the next renewal, and the license keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for. Time already paid for is not refunded.

## Chargebacks and payment disputes {#chargebacks}

Please contact us before you open a chargeback or payment dispute. A dispute ends the license and its subscription at once, and both stay ended whatever the outcome.

## Your legal rights {#legal-rights}

This policy does not limit any right you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you.
