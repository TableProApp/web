TablePro is free and open source, and it stays that way. Every feature that is free today stays free, at work too, with no limit on tabs, windows or connections. The paid plans add optional features to the Mac app, and their prices changed today.

## What changed {#what-changed}

| Plan | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro (was Starter) | $2.99 a month, $24 a year or $59 once, on 2 Macs | $4.99 a month, $39 a year or $79 once, on 3 Macs |
| Team | $1.25 a seat a month, $10 a seat a year or $25 a seat once, from 5 seats | $12 a seat a month, or $10 a seat a month billed yearly, from 2 seats |
| Enterprise | Not offered | On request, at [hello@tablepro.app](mailto:hello@tablepro.app?subject=TablePro%20Enterprise) |

- **Starter is renamed Pro.** It keeps every feature it had, a one-time license is still paid once with no expiry date, and a license now covers 3 Macs instead of 2.
- **Team starts at 2 seats instead of 5.** Each seat covers one activated Mac. Team is a monthly or yearly subscription, and one-time Team seats are no longer sold.
- **Enterprise** is for a company that needs sign-in with its own accounts, enforced policies or an audit log. Write to hello@tablepro.app with the subject "TablePro Enterprise" and say what you need.

## What does not change {#what-stays}

- **Your one-time license** keeps every feature and has no expiry date, and a Pro license bought before today covers 3 Macs too.
- **Your subscription** keeps its price for as long as it renews. Switching it between monthly and yearly moves it to the new price.
- **Your team** keeps its seat price, including seats you add to its subscription. One-time seats stay, and more seats come with a Team subscription.
- **The free app** keeps every feature it has today.

## Why {#why}

Every TablePro release is paid for by licenses and sponsors. The old prices were set when TablePro did far less, and most people who buy a license say they buy it to support the project. The new prices still sit at the low end for a database client.
